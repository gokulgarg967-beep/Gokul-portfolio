03 / Creative thinking

Beauty campaign
concept starters.

Sample concepts created to demonstrate my approach to beauty content. These are proposed ideas, not claims of past brand campaigns.

Sample concept 01 · Short-form video

“Your 10-second beauty switch”

A fast, creator-led transformation format built around one product and one clear use case.

  • Hook: Open with a relatable getting-ready moment.
  • Story: Show the product in use with clear, natural narration.
  • Close: End with the finished look and a simple product takeaway.

Execution idea: Test multiple creator styles and opening hooks, then compare content performance.

Sample concept 02 · UGC / creator brief

“Beauty bag essentials”

A casual, relatable creator format that introduces a product as part of an everyday beauty routine.

  • Hook: “One thing I always keep in my beauty bag…”
  • Story: A natural demo, texture/detail shots and personal-style commentary.
  • Close: A clear product name and audience-friendly call to action.

Execution idea: Share a concise brief with deliverables, key messages, do's/don'ts and timeline.

These concepts are illustrative portfolio samples. No performance figures, client outcomes or Swiss Beauty campaign experience are being claimed.
